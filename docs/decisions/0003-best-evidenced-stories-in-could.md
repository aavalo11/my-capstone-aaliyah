# 0003 · Keep the best-evidenced stories in COULD

**Date.** 2026-09-17
**Status.** Decided, and deliberately uncomfortable

**Context.** W01 raised downloading friction **four separate times, unprompted**, and
volunteered it as the thing worth building. It is by far the strongest evidence in the
research folder. The bridge stories that make up the MUST column, by contrast, have zero
interview support — they are founder-sourced. So the backlog's evidence and its priorities
point in opposite directions.

**Decision.** S1 and S2 (bulk offline download, size-before-download) stay in COULD. The
capstone stays pointed at cross-service sharing. The mismatch is stated out loud in the
backlog rather than smoothed over.

**Why not the alternative.** Repointing the capstone at a download manager would mean
discarding CP-M1 in week 2, on the strength of one interview with one person who isn't
the target user — and "make Spotify's downloader better" is a feature request for
Spotify, not a product I can ship. One participant is not a finding yet.

**What would change my mind.** If W02 and W03 also lead with downloading rather than
sharing, that's three for three and the capstone should move to J1. That decision point
is scheduled before `docs/02-prd.md` is due on 2026-09-27, not after.
