# Expectations — written before looking at any prototype

**Written:** 2026-10-01, before opening `screen-plan.md` or any generated screen.

## What I picture, in my words

> An app that connects music users so they can share albums and play music together.
> You connect your account, and a QR code or link puts people together.

## Screens I expect

- **Connect your account** — S6. First thing you see. Pick your service (Apple Music
  or Spotify) and log in. After that the app knows who you are and what's in your
  library.
- **Share** — S7. Pick an album or a playlist from your library. The app gives you a
  link and a QR code, both big and easy to hand over in person or by text.
- **Join** — S7, S8. Your friend scans the QR code or taps the link. They see what you
  shared and who shared it, and can play it in their own app, whatever service
  they're on. Nothing gets dumped into their library without asking.
- **Together** — S10. Once people are connected they land in one shared space: who's
  in it, what's playing, and everyone can add songs from their own service. This is
  the "play music together" part.
- **What came through** — match report (S7 AC 3–4), S9. When something doesn't
  exist on the other service, you can see which songs are missing and fix a wrong one.

## Stories I can't picture a screen for

S1, S2 (downloading / storage), S3, S4, S11 (DJ stuff), S5 (buy links). They don't fit
the app I picture. Either they belong somewhere I haven't thought of, or they don't
belong in this app.
