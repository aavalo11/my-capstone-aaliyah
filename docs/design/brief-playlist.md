# Brief — recipient view (`prototype/playlist.html`)

The screen my MVP slice turns on (shots 4–5). Taken to real fidelity with the
frontend-design skill on 2026-10-01.

**Who:** A friend on Spotify (often a family plan), on their iPhone, who just tapped a
link Aaliyah texted them. They've never heard of Crossfade and they're opening it inside
Messages.

**The job:** Trust the link enough to tap Play, and know what they'll hear before they do.

**The parts:** Who sent it and the playlist name at the top. A match summary
("23 of 25 on Spotify") with the track strip. One primary "Play in Spotify" button,
nothing competing with it. The full track list below, with missing songs greyed out and
named.

**The feel:** Quick, trustworthy, no homework.

## What the brief changed

- Sender shown first, as the trust signal.
- Track strip enlarged into the hero; big "23" number replaced by a sentence plus running time.
- Play in Spotify pinned to the bottom (thumb reach), with the no-sign-in promise beside it.
- Buy links (S5) removed — the brief says nothing competes with Play.
