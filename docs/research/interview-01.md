# Interview 01 — Discovery Log

Source: my discovery log write-up. One participant, a music app user.
Referenced in `docs/backlog.md` as **I-01**.

## Quotes captured

- "painfully slow"
- "hard to download"
- "wish there were easier downloading"
- "buy instead of rent music"
- "don't pay artists enough"
- "nice to do playlists and jams with other music services"
- "he doesn't mind not being able to... but it could be nice to do"
- "having downloaded songs and some other app allows you to upload them / sync music apps together to play songs together"
- "easy to sync with projects"
- "gets the job done"
- Mentioned playing music at events (no specific tool or process given)

## Gaps I couldn't turn into stories

- The "2 hours a day" answer followed a confusing non-answer, so I'm not fully
  confident in it.
- "Slow" was never defined, so I don't actually know what a fix looks like.
- "Storage" showed up as one random word with no context, so I left it alone
  instead of guessing.
- The event curation story is thin — I never got a specific tool or process.
- I wanted a story about how he discovers new music, but it never came up, so
  there's nothing to base it on.

## Note on evidence strength

The cross-platform playlist quote is hedged in his own words ("doesn't mind not
being able to"). I logged it as a nice-to-have, not a pain point. The download
complaints were the strongest thing he said.
