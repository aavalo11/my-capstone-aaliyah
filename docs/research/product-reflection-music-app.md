# Product Reflection — Music App

## 1. What I Did

The product I chose to use was the Music app on my phone. I use it multiple times a day for hours at a time, depending on what I'm doing. I chose this app because I use it so often. I was going to choose my laptop or my phone but wanted to do an app instead since I felt more of a connection to it.

## 2. Artifact

I reach for the Music app because I love listening to music and different artists. I have a bunch of different playlists for different scenarios and it's fun finding a bunch of artists and connecting with people that have similar tastes. The last time I used it, I just wanted some background music while I was doing homework. If it vanished tomorrow, I would be upset since I use it so much, but I would probably just listen or watch other things to fill the silence.

Interview questions:

1. "When do you usually end up opening your music app?"
2. "Do you have a go to playlist, does it depend on how you're feeling?"
3. "How many times a day or how many hours do you use the app?"

## 3. Claim

"The Music app is a great way to connect with people, it's a great app to use on a day to day basis, and it fits into pretty much anything you're doing, whether that's homework, working out, or just relaxing."

## 4. Question

Do you use the product because you have to or do you use it since everyone has it or is it for fun?

## 5. Reflection

It was fun looking back at all of my playlists and seeing the different vibes that I have. I'm interested in seeing what other people in the class choose for their products. There are so many options to choose from, but I feel like music really represents me and I love listening to it.
